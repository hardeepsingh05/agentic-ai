# Public EV Charging Landscape in Bengaluru, India

## How many public charging points are there?
Bengaluru is one of India’s strongest EV charging markets. A recent industry roundup cited **more than 2,900 public chargers in Bengaluru** spread across malls, office parks, metro stations, and public parking lots. At the state level, Karnataka has been reported at **roughly 5,800–6,800 public charging stations**, depending on the source and date, which is a useful indicator of the city’s depth of infrastructure. For planning purposes, a fleet should treat Bengaluru as a market with **several thousand public charging points**, not a sparse pilot market.

## Two major charging networks a fleet could rely on
**Tata Power EZ Charge** is one of the largest and most established networks in India and has a substantial footprint in Bengaluru. Tata Power says its network spans thousands of public charging points nationwide, and local Bengaluru posts show large hubs with mixed AC/DC capacity. For a fleet operator, the advantage is scale, brand stability, and broad geographic coverage.

**Statiq** is another major network with a fast-growing national presence and an active Bengaluru footprint. Statiq reports **7,000+ charging stations across 63+ cities** in India, and recent Bengaluru listings show new high-power sites in commercial districts. For fleets, Statiq is attractive for app-based discovery, expanding city coverage, and access to both AC and DC charging options.

## Fleet takeaway
Bengaluru now has enough public charging density to support a growing electric sales fleet, especially if charging is supplemented with depot/home charging. The most practical public-network options to evaluate first are **Tata Power EZ Charge** and **Statiq**, given their scale and visible Bengaluru presence.
